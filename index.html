01 Studio

We build worlds worth feeling.

Driftcel is an independent animation studio making original stories with a human pulse and a visual point of view.

Animation comes from the Latin anima: life, soul. From the first sketch to the final frame, we care about the details that make a world feel lived in. A strange little gesture. A colour you can almost hear. A character you recognise before they speak.
