Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

The short version

  • We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website.
  • We only collect what you choose to send us, such as your name, email address and message.
  • We use it to reply to you and to work with you. We never sell it.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to info@driftcel.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how Driftcel Animation (OPC) Private Limited ("Driftcel", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you visit driftcel.com, use our apps and games, or get in touch with us. It is published under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (together, the "DPDP law"), and the Information Technology Act, 2000 with the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

1. Who we are

Driftcel is the "Data Fiduciary" for the personal data described here, which means we decide why and how it is used.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, email address, the type of project and your message.
  • Emails you send us: your name, email address, the content of your email and anything you attach.
  • If we work together: the contact, billing and project details needed to run the project and meet our legal and tax obligations.

Information collected automatically

We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or tracking pixels, and the website does not store anything in your browser. To deliver the website, two providers receive basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested:

  • GitHub, Inc., which hosts the website (GitHub Pages) and may keep server logs for security purposes.
  • Google LLC, which serves our fonts (Google Fonts).

We don't receive these logs or use this information to identify you.

Information we don't want

Please don't send us sensitive information such as passwords, bank or card details, health information or identity documents. We don't need it to reply to you.

3. How we use your data

  • To reply to your message and continue the conversation you started.
  • To discuss and carry out collaborations, projects or work opportunities.
  • To keep records of our conversations and agreements.
  • To meet our legal, accounting and tax obligations.
  • To keep the website and our systems secure.

We don't sell or rent your personal data, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't make automated decisions about you.

4. Why we're allowed to use it

When you contact us, you give us your details voluntarily for a specific purpose: so we can reply. We use them only for that purpose, as permitted under Section 7(a) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Where we need your consent for something else, such as adding you to a newsletter, we will ask first. You can withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by emailing info@driftcel.com. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything we did lawfully before you withdrew it. We also process data where the law requires us to, for example to keep accounting records.

5. How our contact form works

When you press "Send message", the form opens your own email app with your message filled in, and nothing is sent until you send that email yourself. The website doesn't store what you type. If we start using a form-handling service in future, it will process your message on our behalf under a contract, and we will update this policy.

6. Our apps and games

This policy also applies to the apps and games we publish, for example on Google Play, under the developer name Driftcel. The same principles apply: we collect only what an app needs to work, we never sell your data, and you keep all the rights described in this policy.

Each app's store listing includes a data safety section showing exactly what data it collects and why. Where an app collects data beyond what's described here, for example to create an account or save your progress, we will describe it in a section of this policy for that app before it is released.

Our apps are not designed to collect personal data from children. If an app is meant for children, we will follow the DPDP law's rules on children's data and Google Play's Families Policy, including not using behavioural tracking or targeted advertising.

7. Who we share it with

We share personal data only when we need to, and only with:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf, such as our email and website hosting providers, under contracts that require them to protect it.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers and accountants, who are bound by confidentiality.
  • Government authorities or courts, when the law requires it.

8. Data stored outside India

Some of our service providers, including GitHub and Google, may store or process data on servers outside India, for example in the United States. We transfer data outside India only as permitted under the DPDP law and any restrictions notified by the Government of India.

9. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: for as long as we need them to reply and follow up. We delete them within 24 months of our last contact unless we're working together.
  • Project, contract and billing records: for as long as Indian law requires, which is generally 8 years for accounting records.

When we no longer need personal data, we delete it, or anonymise it if we need it for records.

10. How we protect it

We use reasonable security practices to protect personal data, including HTTPS encryption across the website, limiting access to the people who need it, and protecting our accounts with strong passwords and two-factor authentication where available. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP law requires.

11. Your rights

Under the DPDP law you can:

  • Access: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, how we use it and who we've shared it with.
  • Correct or update data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Erase: ask us to delete your data when we no longer need it or you withdraw consent, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.
  • Raise a grievance with us and get a response.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to.

To use any of these rights, email info@driftcel.com with the subject "Privacy request". We may need to confirm your identity first. We will respond within 30 days.

12. Delete your data or account

You can ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you, including any account you have in a Driftcel app or game, at any time:

  1. Email info@driftcel.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Tell us the email address you used to contact us and, for an app, the app's name and your username or account email.
  3. We'll confirm the request comes from you, then delete your account and personal data within 30 days and let you know when it's done.

You don't need to keep the app installed to make this request. We may keep a small amount of information if the law requires it, such as billing records, for the period the law sets. We delete it after that.

13. Children

This website is not directed at children. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you. Under the DPDP law we need the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian before we process a child's personal data. If we learn that we have collected a child's data without that consent, we will delete it.

14. Grievance Officer

If you have a question or complaint about how we handle personal data, please contact our Grievance Officer:

Grievance Officer, Driftcel Animation (OPC) Private Limited

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website or the law changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. If we make a significant change, we'll make it clear on this website.

16. Contact us
